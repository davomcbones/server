Closed/Demolished Malls

Waterfront Shopping Center

Date built: Approx 2014

Date closed: Approx 2018, and 2024

Location: Roblox

Original owner: Unknown

Waterfront Shopping Center is an ancient relic of the old Roblox, believed to be built in around 2014, featured many areas and was prominent in the Roblox lift community. However it has experienced decline in 2018 due to age and eventually shuttered after the owner's Roblox account was deleted. In 2021, there was an attempt to restore the mall, but the project fell short due to the mall's outdated and wacky dimensions rendering the restoration impractical. Waterfront was abandoned for the second time in 2024 10 years after it's supposed construction date.

Brubalo Plaza (Shopping district)

Date built: Approx 2024 (Lore: 1970)

Date closed: 24th November, 2025

Location: brub's plot, Bloxburg

Original owner: Brubalo Plaza LTD.

A centerpiece in the 1970's, the Brubalo Plaza is a multi-use hotel building with a shopping space on the ground floor. It was primarily visited by the fellow residents and hotel goers of the plaza. Unfortunatley however, Brubalo Plaza faced a similar fate of PineView mall due to a lack of maintenance and modernization. The hotel quickly grew out of fashion, stores relocated, and customer counts declined. In 2024 the Brubalo Plaza has shuttered it's doors, leaving TacoBucket the only remaining tenant and later inherited owner of the building. TacoBucket relocated from the shopping space to the hotel's former pool room in front of the property. TacoBucket remained open for another year, still tethered to the now abandoned hotel, before being aqcuired by Bangals Center Group and later demolished in 2025.

Cobblestone Mall

Date closed: Approx early 2026

However the loss in customers due to Roblox's controversal change in age restrictions became the final name in the coffin, aquired by Bangal Centers Group in the new year of 2026, the future of this mall remains uncertain.
